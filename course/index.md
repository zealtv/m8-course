---
Twelve weeks at about five hours a week, for someone who reads music fluently,
programs, and can already build a short loop on the M8. It teaches how to make
the instrument carry long, developed pieces, and how to build its sounds from
the synthesis engines.

The course runs three strands side by side: **sound** (FM, drums, pianos,
bells, basses), **composition** (rhythm, variation, counterpoint, harmony) and
**form** (a method for planning and finishing pieces longer than a loop). Each
week ends with an etude whose length grows from 24 bars to a full piece.
