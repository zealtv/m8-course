---
title: "Appendices"
---
Reference tables and blank worksheets to use alongside the weekly modules.
They are written for print as well as the screen.

Planned contents: a command quick reference grouped by musical purpose; hex,
tick and tempo arithmetic tables; an FM ratio and algorithm crib; a glossary of
tracker and M8 terms; and blank worksheets for patches, phrases, chains and
songs, one-page form plans and self-assessment.
