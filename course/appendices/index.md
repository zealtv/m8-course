---
title: "Appendices"
---
Reference tables and blank worksheets to use alongside the weekly modules.
They are written for print as well as the screen.

The reference pages: a command quick reference grouped by musical purpose;
hex, tick and tempo arithmetic; an FM ratio and algorithm crib with
starting-point recipes; a glossary of tracker and M8 terms; and suggestions
for further reading and listening. Then the blank worksheets, one page
each: an instrument patch sheet, a phrase grid, a chain and song plan, a
one-page form plan and a self-assessment rubric.
