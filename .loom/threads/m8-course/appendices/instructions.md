# Appendices and worksheets

Written in our own words and laid out for print:

- Command quick reference grouped by musical purpose (time, pitch, variation,
  structure, mix), with page references to the manual.
- Hex, tick and tempo arithmetic tables; groove recipes with working shown;
  least-common-multiple table for polymeter planning.
- FM ratio and algorithm crib for the sound families in weeks 2-4.
- Glossary of tracker and M8 terms.
- Blank worksheets: instrument patch sheet, phrase grid, chain and song
  plan, one-page form plan, self-assessment rubric.
- Further reading and listening, described without linking to anything that
  identifies the author.
