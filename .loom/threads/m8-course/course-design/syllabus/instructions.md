# Syllabus

Write `docs/syllabus.md` from the draft outline in the goal stitch and the
manual notes.

- Course-level learning outcomes (6-8, each assessable).
- Per week: outcomes, manual sections to read, the lab (sound design or
  technique), the etude (composition, with a duration target and a formal
  plan), and about how the 5 hours divide.
- The form strand: state the framework the etudes build (for example:
  material inventory, a one-page formal plan before sequencing, section
  contrast by one changed dimension at a time, planned transitions), and the
  duration target per week rising from 16 bars to a 3-5 minute piece.
- Assessment for self-study: a rubric per etude, two checkpoint pieces
  (about weeks 4 and 8), a final piece, and a portfolio (instrument library,
  etudes, plans).
- Prerequisite map between weeks.

Check the plan against the manual notes: every technique a week relies on
must be something the notes show the instrument doing.

Show the syllabus to the learner and get their agreement before the module
stitches start.
