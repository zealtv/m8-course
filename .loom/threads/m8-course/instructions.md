# M8 course: a public, printable, university-style course site

Outcome: a static website (Markdown + Eleventy, deployed to GitHub Pages) that
teaches the Dirtywave M8 as a 12-week course at about 5 hours a week, for a
learner who reads music fluently, programs, and already makes 4-8 bar loops on
the M8.

Decisions the learner made on 2026-11-12:

- Start point: already making loops. Foundations become a diagnostic review.
- Pace: 12 weeks, about 5 h/week, one final piece.
- Sound scope: synthesis first (FM Synth, Wavsynth, Macrosynth, Hypersynth).
  The Sampler appears only for resampling and render-to-sample.
- Stack: Markdown + Eleventy, GitHub Pages, print stylesheet.

Stated aims: sound design (FM, drums, pianos, bells, vibes, basses);
composition with counterpoint, variation, polymeter and polyrhythm; a
framework of exercises for getting beyond 4-8 bar loops; thorough, structured
coverage of the instrument.

Constraints:

- Anonymised. No names, handles, email addresses, analytics or personal
  detail anywhere in the published site or its metadata.
- Printable: each module on its own, and the whole course as one reader.
- Source: the operation manual that arrived on 2026-11-12 (cover reads
  "Version 6.5.2 04/21/2026"). Cite it by section and page; write original
  prose and draw original diagrams. Do not reproduce its text or figures.
- Unofficial: the site says it has no affiliation with the manufacturer.

Draft outline, to be settled in `syllabus`:

| Wk | Topic | Strand |
|----|-------|--------|
| 1 | The M8 as a compositional system; diagnostic and baseline piece | foundations |
| 2 | FM I: operators, ratios, algorithms; bells and vibes | sound |
| 3 | FM II: electric pianos and basses; modulation and expression | sound |
| 4 | Drum synthesis across the engines; tables as micro-sequencers | sound |
| 5 | Time: ticks, grooves, tuplets, polyrhythm | rhythm |
| 6 | Polymeter: unequal phrase and chain lengths, phase cycles | rhythm |
| 7 | Variation: motivic transformation, conditional and chance commands | composition |
| 8 | Counterpoint for eight monophonic voices | composition |
| 9 | Harmony and texture: chords, voicing, voice budget, pads | composition/sound |
| 10 | Form: planning long pieces, sections, transitions | form |
| 11 | Mix, effects, automation, resampling and render | production |
| 12 | Final piece and portfolio | capstone |

A form strand runs through every week: each week's etude has a longer
duration target and a stated formal plan, so the loop habit gets worked on
from week 1, not only in week 10.
